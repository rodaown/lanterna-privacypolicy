Halloween assets

Decorations for Halloween week. They're drawn in code in the same style as the game (same colours, same square size), so this is how they look in the app, animated as in the game.

In the maze

Rule: decorations go on the walls only, never on the squares you walk on. Pumpkins sit in small niches carved into the wall, the skeleton is carved into the stone (faded), cobwebs are see-through in the corners. They don't bob or sparkle like gems and items, so they never look like something to pick up. Use the buttons to compare.

Game elements

In the app's screens

The main menu: cobwebs in the corners, a pumpkin instead of the flame, the subtitle "Halloween Night", bats flying past and a skeleton peeking from the bottom.
