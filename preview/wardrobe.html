Sparky's wardrobe

Cosmetics only: they don't change light, speed or difficulty, so the leaderboard stays fair. They're won at random in the crypts and worn from the Inventory ("Wardrobe" tab). Drawn as in the game, seen from above.

Try the combinations

Hoods with a crest

The whole hood changes colour, with a crest on the back of the head (seen from above, like the rest of the character).

Lanterns

Flame colour

No blue flame: in the game the light turns blue when a Shadow is close (fear), and a blue flame would blur that warning. The silver flame takes its place.

Trails

Companions

Little friends who follow Sparky a few steps behind. Cosmetic only: they give no light, don't scare the Shadows and don't touch anything, so they can't be mistaken for Shadows or for things to pick up.

Chances in the crypts

Every time you clear a crypt (with the Guardian or a secret one) there's a 50% chance that a gift appears: a cosmetic you don't own yet, which you can pick up or leave. Which one depends on rarity: common weight 6, uncommon weight 3, rare weight 1. The percentages below are per crypt cleared when you don't own anything yet; as you find them, the remaining ones become more likely.
