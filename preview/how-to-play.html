The story

The street lamps of Borgo Cinerea are going out one by one. Master Ottavio Mori, the old lamplighter, went down the Old Well after the light and left only a note: "Don't follow me." His apprentice, Sparky, follows him anyway, with nothing but a lantern.

Each level opens with a short note, and pages of the master's diary are hidden by the bonfires underground, one per level. The story is told in acts: Act I · The Descent and Act II · The Crystal Mines are in the game, Act III is coming.

The basics

Goal

You're in a dark maze with a lantern: you only see what it lights up, and the places you've visited stay on the map in half-light. Find the exit, the green door .

Controls

  • Swipe: the character runs straight and stops at the first crossing or at a wall.
  • Arrows at the bottom right: one step at a time; hold to keep going.
  • Two fingers: zoom the maze. Double-tap to go back to normal size.
  • Map: everything you've explored. Two fingers to zoom, one finger to move it.
  • ☰ or Back button: pause, with Resume, Restart and All levels.

New to the game? The Tutorial in the main menu teaches everything in 9 short chapters (about 2 minutes) with free aids.

The Shadows

The Shadows

They sleep in the dark, invisible, and wake up when your lantern's light reaches them. When one is near, the flame trembles and turns colder, and you hear a heartbeat that speeds up as you get closer. They are slower than you when you run: if you lose them, they stop and wait where they last saw you. If they catch you, you start again from the last lit bonfire, and the explored map stays.

The button turns the light off: you walk in the dark seeing only the map you remember, you wake no one and the Shadows can't catch you. But in the dark you don't discover new places, and the dark doesn't last: you can take at most 15 steps, then the lantern relights by itself. The bar on the button shows how many steps are left; it refills as you walk with the light on.

Kinds of Shadows

  • Shadow (purple, red eyes): sleeps in the dark and slowly drifts around its nest. It wakes up when your lantern's light reaches it and chases you as long as it sees you; if you lose it, it stops and prowls where it lost you.
  • Huntress (blue, small horns, light-blue eyes): in the crystal mines. Once awake it follows you everywhere, even if you hide, and it's a bit faster than the others. In the dark it can't catch you but stays right behind you: free it with a flash, a bonfire or fireflies.
  • Guardian (red, crown of horns, golden eyes): rises at the end of every crypt. It's big and slow, but it always follows you and one hit isn't enough: it takes 3 hits of light (flash, brazier, living flame, fireflies). It's cunning: it steers clear of lit braziers, isn't lured by fireflies, dashes at you when it sees you in a straight line and, when wounded, sometimes calls a Shadow from the tombs. Once freed, it leaves the exit key.

Light, gems and items

Gems, flashes and bonfires

  • Gems: every level starts with 1 flash, and each gem you collect is worth another one (round button at the bottom left). The flash frees forever the nearby Shadows you can see.
  • Bonfires: walk over one to light it, and it becomes your restart point. A lit bonfire frees the Shadows that pass next to it: you can lure them there.
  • Key: in some levels the exit door is padlocked. Find the key first, then get out.
  • Help with a video (always optional): an extra flash whenever you run out of flashes, always available; a 20-second trail to the nearest gem, up to 3 times per level; plus, once per level, the compass: an arrow that points to the nearest gem as the crow flies until the end of the level. In levels with a key, until you find it the compass points to the key; once you have the key it switches off, and the gem compass takes another video.

In the crystal mines

  • Crystals: when your lantern light touches them they light up for good and brighten their surroundings. Careful: their light opens the eyes of nearby Shadows.
  • Living flame: pick it up and tap the button to throw it where you're facing. It flies up to 5 cells and burns for 15 seconds: it reveals the dark, but wakes the Shadows it lights up.
  • Fireflies: set the jar down and move away. Nearby Shadows go to it instead of you and stay spellbound beside it: after 15 seconds the jar bursts and its light frees them.

The crypts

The crypts

Between some levels there's a crypt: when you go in, the doors close and waves of Shadows rise from the tombs. The crypt is fully lit and the lantern can't be put out; there's always a gem to pick up and a new one appears every 10 seconds: each one is a flash. Light the braziers by walking over them: they free Shadows passing by. At the end the Guardian rises: free it with 3 hits of light (flash, lit brazier, living flame, fireflies); each brazier hits it once and then goes out. The Guardian leaves the exit key and a reward: a life, two flashes, a living flame and fireflies. If you're caught inside, the crypt starts over. Some levels also hide a secret crypt, off the way to the exit: it's optional, there's no Guardian, and freeing the Shadows that rise from the tombs wins you 3 or 4 flashes. You also win an object for your bag: oil flask, little bell, soot cloak or vial of darkness. Sometimes, after clearing a crypt, a gift appears too: a cosmetic for your Wardrobe (looks only), which you can pick up or leave.

Guardian crypts (✠)Levels of their own between the others (after levels 3, 5 and 9). Two waves of Shadows, then the Guardian. Mandatory: the Guardian has the exit key. Reward: a life, two flashes, a living flame and fireflies.
Secret cryptsA small hidden room inside levels 4, 7, 8 and 10, away from the route to the exit. Optional: one wave, no Guardian. Reward: 3 or 4 flashes and an object for your bag.

The inventory

Objects you find stay with you from one level to the next (flashes don't: they belong to the level). Open Inventory → Weapons from the main menu to see them, or tap the bag button at the bottom of the screen during a level to use them (the game pauses while the bag is open). You can carry up to 5 of each. After a final Game Over the bag is emptied, together with the run.

Living flameThrow it ahead: it flies 5 squares, lights the dark and frees the Shadows it touches. Found in Act II levels.
Firefly jarPut it down: nearby Shadows stay spellbound until the jar bursts and frees them. Found in Act II levels.
Oil flaskFor 30 seconds your light reaches one square further. Found in secret crypts.
Little bellShadows within 5 squares stand still for 8 seconds (the Guardian only 3). Found in secret crypts.
Soot cloakWorks by itself: the next time a Shadow catches you, you don't lose a life. Found in secret crypts.
Vial of darknessRefills the 15 steps you can take with the lantern off. Found in secret crypts.

The wardrobe

Every crypt you clear has a 50% chance of leaving a gift: a cosmetic you don't own yet. Step on it to see it, then pick it up (and wear it) or leave it. Cosmetics only change how Sparky looks: hoods with a crest, lanterns, flame colours, trails and companions. Wear them from Inventory → Wardrobe. See them all in the wardrobe preview →

Lives, stars and score

Stars and lives

You have up to 3 lives ♥: you lose one every time a Shadow catches you and earn one for each level you complete. At zero lives it's Game Over: with a video you restart the level with 1 life, otherwise you start again from level 1. Finishing a level unlocks the next one. 3 stars if you use at most 125% of the steps of the shortest path, 2 stars up to double, otherwise 1.

At the end of each level you get points. The run total (all levels since your last Game Over) is your score on the Google Play Games leaderboard.
